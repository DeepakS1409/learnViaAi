ABOUT THE PLATFORM

Empowering Higher Education with Grounded AI

Padika AI bridges the gap between official college curriculum and autonomous student self-study through accurate Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

✦ Our Mission: Anti-Hallucination Academic Tutoring

Standard public AI models often invent answers or draw from conflicting international syllabi. Padika AI is fundamentally different: every question asked by a student is vector-searched against the exact documents uploaded by their designated course professor.

If a topic is not in the professor's notes, Padika AI explicitly alerts the student rather than guessing. Furthermore, it offers multilingual support in English, தமிழ், and colloquial Tanglish, ensuring no language barrier stands between students and exam success.

Enterprise-Grade Academic Tech Stack

⚡

Python Flask & SQLite

Lightweight, performant backend with clean relational models designed for effortless migration to PostgreSQL.

📄

PyMuPDF (fitz)

High-fidelity PDF document parser that extracts content page-by-page to guarantee 100% accurate page citations.

🧠

Multilingual Embeddings

Cross-lingual dense vectorization supporting English, Tamil Unicode, and Romanized Tanglish subwords.

🔍

Cosine Vector Search

Fast nearest-neighbor semantic search scoped strictly by student's department, semester, and subject code.

🤖

Gemini RAG Engine

State-of-the-art context processing delivering 2-mark definitions, 13-mark answers, and 16-mark structured guides.

🎨
